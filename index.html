05 / Verify offline

TAKE THE EVIDENCE WITH YOU.

Once the evidence bundle is available, verification can run offline.

  1. 01

    AI APPLICATION / PRODUCTION

    A supported capture path records interaction data.

  2. 02

    CAPTURED EVIDENCE (via AELITIUM)

    The captured material is stored in an evidence bundle.

    ai_canonical.json ai_manifest.json
  3. 03

    TRANSFER

    Copy the bundle to a different environment.

  4. 04

    OFFLINE REVIEW

    Check the available evidence offline.

aelitium verify-bundle ./bundle

No production access is required for this verification step once the bundle is available. The bundle carries the recorded evidence and supporting data used by AELITIUM’s verification and comparison contracts. Additional trust/policy inputs may be supplied locally; the bundle is not itself a trusted external trust store.

07

07 / Boundaries

EVIDENCE HAS A BOUNDARY.
WE MAKE IT VISIBLE.

AELITIUM checks the recorded evidence. It does not establish that the AI’s answer is true or that every interaction was captured.

What AELITIUM can check

Consistency checks on the supplied evidence

  • Payload integrity
  • Binding field consistency
  • Invocation identity consistency
  • Invocation binding consistency

Absent evidence and unevaluated properties are reported separately.

Additional checks when the required evidence or policy inputs are supplied

Signature validity
When signature evidence and verification material are present.
Trusted signer identity
When a valid signature’s key matches an explicitly supplied external trust store.
Declared-time freshness
With an explicit maximum age and UTC reference time. Checks the declared canonical timestamp, not historical time.
What it does not establish

By itself, evidence verification does not establish:

  • Provider execution.
  • Response causation.
  • Capture completeness.
  • Output truth, correctness or safety.
  • Complete real-world invocation identity.
  • Historical occurrence.
  • Trusted historical time.
  • Authorization.
  • Legal compliance.

In v0.4.0, authorization is always NOT_EVALUATED.

EVIDENCE SHOULD SUPPORT A CONCLUSION.
IT SHOULD NOT SILENTLY BECOME A STRONGER ONE.
